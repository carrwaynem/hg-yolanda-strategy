Protecting Your Mother's Estate
⚖️ Power of Attorney (POA)

Establish POA on your mother's financial accounts now, before it is needed. Without it, if she becomes unable to make decisions, you would need to go through the courts to access or manage her funds; a process that is slow, expensive, and entirely avoidable. This one step protects both of you.

🏛️ Trust vs. Will: Why It Matters

A will is a request; it still goes through probate court, which takes time, costs money, and is public record. A trust is a legal structure that holds assets and passes them directly to beneficiaries with no court involvement, no delays, and no public record. If your mother has a will but not a trust, the trust is the next conversation.
